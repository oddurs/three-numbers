## 2026-09-11

**Method.** From the pilot's measured rate, set a weekly word target and put the milestone due dates on it. The dates in this plan are a guess until one chapter has been written end to end; replace them with the measured rate and say so.

## 2026-09-12

**Cadence, recalibrated — with one number still unmeasured.**

The pilot recalibrated *scope*: 130,600 planned words became 90,950, about 227 pages of prose. Dates recomputed from that, part by part.

**The rate is an assumption, not a measurement.** 2,000 finished words per week at this density — a realistic part-time figure for technical prose that carries derivations and computed figures. I cannot honestly measure a human writing rate from this pilot, and pretending otherwise would make every date downstream a fiction dressed as a finding.

**What to do after Chapter 1.** Record the wall-clock hours for each stage on its item. After three chapters there is a real rate; replace the 2,000 with it and rerun this. Figure work is the most likely thing to break the estimate: the half-grey figure took a meaningful fraction of the pilot, and Parts IV and V need many more figures per chapter than Part II did.
