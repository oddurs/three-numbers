## 2026-09-11

**The argument.** Additive mixing is the easy case: two lights add, and the arithmetic is linear. A surface does not add anything. It removes, multiplicatively, and then the ink sits in a layer with thickness and scatter and a substrate underneath. This chapter is the one place the book leaves the comfort of a three-by-three matrix, and it is the reason CMYK is not a colour space.
