## 2026-09-11

**Scope.** Every derivation, every number, every claim about a standard.

**Method.** Find readers who will disagree: someone from colour science, someone from graphics, someone from print. Give them the PDF and the repository, because the strongest form of review here is 'your figure is wrong, here is the patch'.

This is the single highest-risk item in the plan. A book that computes everything is falsifiable in a way most books are not, which is the point and also the exposure.
