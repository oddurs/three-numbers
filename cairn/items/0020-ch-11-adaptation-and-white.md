## 2026-09-11

**The argument.** The eye re-normalises. A sheet of paper reads as white under tungsten and under noon daylight, though the light reaching the eye differs by a factor of three in the blue. Modelling that re-normalisation is chromatic adaptation, and it is the one piece of genuine perceptual modelling that ordinary pipelines cannot avoid.
