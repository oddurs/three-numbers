## 2026-09-11

**The argument.** Treat spectral power distributions as what they are --- elements of a function space --- and the operations that matter become familiar. Reflection is pointwise multiplication. Mixing lights is addition. Measurement is an inner product. The only unusual thing is that the space is infinite-dimensional and the measurements number three.
