## Why it matters

The source of every measured dataset in `data/`: the CIE colorimetric observers,
the Judd–Vos modification, the Stockman & Sharpe cone fundamentals and the
illuminant SPDs. Maintained at the UCL Institute of Ophthalmology, and the
closest thing the field has to a canonical machine-readable archive.

## Claims

- [high | cmfs/ciexyz31.csv] {#cmf-tables} The CIE 1931 2° observer is tabulated from 360 to 830 nm at 5 nm intervals. #data
- [high | cones/linss2_10e_5.csv] {#ss-cone-table} The Stockman & Sharpe 2° cone fundamentals are tabulated in linear energy units from 390 nm, with blank cells above ~615 nm where the S-cone response falls below the measurement floor. #data
- [medium | cie/Illuminantd65.csv] {#d65-table} The D65 relative SPD is tabulated from 300 to 830 nm at 1 nm. #data
