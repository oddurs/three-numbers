## Why it matters

The contrast requirement that is written into accessibility law in several
jurisdictions, and therefore the formula that actually governs what ships.
Chapter 19 treats it as a standard to be understood rather than mocked, while
being precise about where it fails.

## Claims

- [high | SC 1.4.3] {#wcag-ratio} Contrast is defined as (L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05) where L is relative luminance, with a 4.5:1 threshold for normal text and 3:1 for large text. #contrast
- [high | SC 1.4.11] {#wcag-nontext} Non-text contrast for UI components and graphical objects requires 3:1 against adjacent colours. #contrast
- [medium | Understanding 1.4.3] {#wcag-flat-model} The formula takes no account of font size beyond a single large-text threshold, nor of polarity, nor of the surround. #contrast
