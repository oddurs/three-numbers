## Claims

- [high | §results] {#dress-bimodal} Observers' reports of the dress photograph are bimodal rather than continuously distributed, splitting into white-gold and blue-black groups. #adaptation
- [high | §discussion] {#dress-illuminant-assumption} The split is best explained by differing assumptions about the illuminant: observers who assume a bluish daylight discount blue and see white-gold. #adaptation
- [medium | §discussion] {#dress-underdetermined} The image genuinely underdetermines the illuminant, so neither percept is an error. #adaptation
