## Why it matters

The reference work for the experimental foundations. Where CIE 015 states a
definition, Wyszecki and Stiles explain the experiment that produced it and
tabulate the data. Essential for Chapters 3 and 4, where the book needs the
colour-matching experiment in enough detail to explain the negative lobes.

## Claims

- [high | ch. 3] {#negative-matches} Some spectral test stimuli cannot be matched by any non-negative combination of three real primaries; the experimenter must desaturate the test field, which is recorded as a negative primary value. #cmf
- [high | ch. 3] {#grassmann-laws} Colour matching obeys additivity and proportionality — Grassmann's laws — which is an empirical finding, not a necessity, and it is what licenses the linear-algebraic treatment of colour. #linearity
- [medium | ch. 3] {#fundamental-metamer} Any stimulus decomposes uniquely into a fundamental component, which determines the tristimulus values, and a metameric black, which the observer cannot detect at all. #metamerism
