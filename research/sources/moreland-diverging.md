## Claims

- [high | §3] {#diverging-needs-neutral} A diverging map must pass through a light neutral at its midpoint, or the two halves cannot be read as symmetric about a meaningful zero. #palette
- [high | §2] {#rainbow-fails} The rainbow colour map introduces false gradients at hue boundaries and has non-monotone lightness, so it misrepresents the data it displays. #palette
