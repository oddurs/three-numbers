## Claims

- [high | §2] {#brettel-halfplanes} A dichromat's reduced colour gamut is modelled as two half-planes in LMS space, hinged on the line joining the origin to the white point. #cvd
- [high | §2] {#brettel-anchors} The two half-planes are anchored at 475 nm and 575 nm for protanopia and deuteranopia, and at 485 nm and 660 nm for tritanopia. #cvd
- [high | §3] {#brettel-preserves-white} The projection leaves the neutral axis unchanged, so greys are invariant under simulation — a useful correctness check on any implementation. #cvd
